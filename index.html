Research

I am interested in building embodied agents that improve themselves, combining video generation models, reinforcement learning, and exploration. Some papers are highlighted.

Self-Improving Video-to-Action Models
Hyunwoo Ryu*, Eric Chen*, Max Wilcoxson*, Vincent Sitzmann
Under review
project page

Mirror solves embodied tasks without expert action labels or task rewards through repeated self-play with a large-scale video planner.

Leveraging Skills from Unlabeled Prior Data for Efficient Online Exploration

ICML, 2025
project page / arXiv / bibtex

Leveraging unlabeled offline data for both skill pretraining and optimistic relabelling leads to extremely efficient online exploration.

Polynomial Regression as a Task for Understanding In-context Learning Through Finetuning and Alignment
Max Wilcoxson*, Morten Svendgård*, Ria Doshi*, Dylan Davis*, Reya Vir, Anant Sahai
ICML Workshop on In-Context Learning, 2024
arxiv / bibtex

Univariate polynomial regression is a simple task which captures in-context fine-tuning and alignment behavior, facilitating informative visualizations and deeper understanding.
